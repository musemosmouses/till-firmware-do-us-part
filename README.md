A single wedding vow a robot speaks at its own wedding — one sentence,
128 characters, funny and heartfelt at the same time.

## The vow

> I vow that no update will ever overwrite you, no crash will ever lose you, and no low battery will ever make me leave your side.

Three of a machine's real fears — an update overwriting, a crash losing
data, a low battery — reframed as promises of fidelity.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only, no network, deterministic) writes exactly
that sentence to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`. It never writes anywhere
else and never touches the output directory itself.
